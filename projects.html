What this project is about

A collection of the research journey behind my Master's work — from the mathematical foundations of chaos to numerical experiments and visualisations.

  • Novel 4D no-equilibrium hyperchaotic system
  • Two positive Lyapunov exponents and hyperchaotic dynamics
  • No-equilibrium / hidden-attractor behaviour
  • Kaplan–Yorke dimension, phase portraits and dynamical analysis
  • Synchronization and chaos-based image encryption
