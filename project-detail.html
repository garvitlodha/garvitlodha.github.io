A Novel 4D No-Equilibrium Hyperchaotic System with Applications in Synchronization and Image Encryption

M.Sc. Mathematics  |  Delhi Technological University  |  2024–2026

Dissertation completed Manuscript in preparation

Overview

This dissertation introduces and analyzes a novel four-dimensional no-equilibrium hyperchaotic system. The work focuses on its dynamical behavior and explores applications in synchronization and image encryption.
